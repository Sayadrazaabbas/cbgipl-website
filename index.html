Who We Are

A Global Vision, Rooted in India

CBGIPL is a Saudi-India Joint Venture backed by Carter Brown Global Holding, combining international financial strength with deep local expertise to shape Mumbai's real estate landscape.

Our Vision

To become a world-class real estate company known for quality, integrity, and commitment to excellence in every project we undertake.

Our Mission

To create value within families, communities, and the environment by providing world-class infrastructure through global-standard projects.

Core Values

Quality, Integrity, Commitment, and Excellence — the four pillars that define every decision we make and every structure we build.

The CBG Story

Founded by Mohammad Nusrat Ali Khan, an engineer and techno-commercial specialist with over a decade of international experience across the Middle East, India, and China, Carter Brown Global has grown into a diversified multinational group.

With established entities in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Hong Kong, and now India, CBG brings the financial muscle and global construction expertise of its international operations to address one of India's most pressing needs — urban transformation.

CBGIPL represents the next chapter: leveraging Saudi capital and international project management standards to deliver world-class real estate developments in Mumbai — from SRA redevelopment projects transforming communities to mega integrated townships redefining urban living.
