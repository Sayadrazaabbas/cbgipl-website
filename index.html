Who We Are

A Global Vision, Rooted in India

CBGIPL is a Mumbai-headquartered private limited company incorporated in India in 2026. Its platform combines development and EPC with global commodity trading.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized infrastructure and real estate company, delivering landmark developments and sustainable economic value.

Our Mission

To develop urban infrastructure and real estate assets, facilitate efficient trade in essential commodities, and build long-term stakeholder value.

Core Values

Integrity, innovation, execution excellence, long-term partnerships and disciplined project and transaction governance.

The CBG Story

Founded by Mohammad Nusrat Ali Khan, CBGIPL combines techno-commercial experience across trading, real estate and commodities with a focus on infrastructure development.

From its Mumbai base, the company draws on group relationships and business networks across the Middle East, Asia and Europe for project and commodity opportunities.

Its two connected areas of focus are physical infrastructure and essential commodity flows, pursued through careful commercial and regulatory review.
