OUR GLOBAL PRESENCE

Carter Brown Global Holding Company operates across multiple countries, delivering excellence in EPC, energy, real estate, facility management, and global trading. Our companies are strategically located in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Hong Kong, and India, ensuring seamless services worldwide.

OUR COMPANIES

📍 KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA

CBICC is a General Contracting Company based in the Eastern Region of Saudi Arabia, specializing in contracting and construction work for infrastructure projects, building construction, MEP, and renewable energy projects. The company collaborates with international partners in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030.

RTGCC – Ruh Thabitah General Contracting Company is a General Contracting Company based in Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia. Formerly known as AYT Electrical Ltd, the company specializes in infrastructure construction, building construction, and electrical works, including substations, power plants, process industries, underground and overhead transmission lines, and renewable energy projects such as solar, wind, and hydrogen power. RTGCC collaborates with local and international companies through joint ventures, consortiums, and strategic partnerships.

📍UNITED ARAB EMIRATES (UAE)

Carter Brown Global – Dubai has vast experience in assessing the supply & demand of a variety of commodities, including precious & base metals, minerals, fertilizers, energy solutions, machinery, equipment, and agricultural products. This knowledge extends across multiple markets.

At CBG – Sharjah, our primary objective is to conduct an in-depth analysis of the demand and supply of petroleum and petrochemical products across various markets along with trade of different types of fertilizers, including nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium-based fertilizers.

📍SULTANATE OF OMAN

SATCC – Shashar Arabian Trading Company: SATCC is based in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman, and is engaged in trading activities, including the export and import of oil, gas, fertilizers, and chemicals.

📍HONG KONG

Carter Brown Global (Hong Kong) Ltd is based in Hong Kong and engaged in the trading of oil, gas, fertilizers, metals, and minerals to cater to the markets of China and Central Asia.

📍 INDIA

Ascendcore Advisory LLP, based in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, is a premier sustainability consulting and project advisory firm. Backed by experts including retired IAS, IFS, IPS officers, bankers, engineers, and legal professionals, we provide strategic solutions in sustainability, digital transformation, and compliance to help organizations drive responsible growth and long-term impact.

Carter Brown Global Pvt Ltd, Mumbai is a sister concern of Carter Brown Global, with 30+ years of industry expertise. In 2023, it launched Al Naba for premium women’s wear and NextGen Uniforms for customized industrial uniforms, expanding into the garment sector with a focus on quality and innovation.

Carter Brown Global Pvt Ltd, Mumbai is a sister concern of Carter Brown Global, with 30+ years of industry expertise. In 2023, it launched Al Naba for premium women’s wear and NextGen Uniforms for customized industrial uniforms, expanding into the garment sector with a focus on quality and innovation.

With a robust international presence, Carter Brown Global Holding Company continues to expand its expertise and deliver innovative solutions across industries.
